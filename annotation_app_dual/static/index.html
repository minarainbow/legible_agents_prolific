Agent Action Annotation

You'll watch an AI use a computer

Imagine an AI controlling a real computer the way a person would — moving the mouse, clicking, typing, scrolling — to finish a task someone asked it to do. That's a computer-use agent.

You'll watch short screen recordings. For each step, you'll answer two questions: how the agent interacted, and what changed.

It will take about 35–50 minutes.

Enter the Prolific ID you use to receive payment. It is normally filled in automatically when you open the study from Prolific.

How it works

This is not a test of you

We are not evaluating how well you annotate. Some steps are genuinely hard — or even impossible — to see clearly from the video. That is expected.

  • If you can't tell, say Can't tell. That is a useful answer.
  • You are not failing the study when a step is unclear.
  • Please don't pick Can't tell only to finish faster — but do use it when the step is truly unclear. We'll sanity-check answers at the end.
  • Do not search the web or use AI tools. We are measuring how hard the steps are to understand — not your annotating skill. Being incorrect or unsure is completely okay; we want honest answers.
  • Replay the clip as many times as you want.

What you'll do

  1. See the task the agent was asked to do.
  2. Watch the recording — it pauses after each action.
  3. Answer two questions for that step (each with confidence).
  4. A few wrap-up questions after each recording.

Practice starts with a few example answers already filled in — you only annotate the last step yourself.

Progress saves automatically.
